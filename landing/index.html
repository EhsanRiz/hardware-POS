    Why this matters here

    Built for the connection you actually have.

    A till that needs the internet is a till that closes when the internet does. In a Free State border town or a Maseru side street, that is not a rare event — it is a Tuesday.

    Sell through the outage

    The price list lives on the tablet. Staff carry on scanning, pricing and printing while the line is down; nobody stands at the counter waiting for a spinner.

    Reconcile without duplicates

    Every sale carries a key generated on the device. If a request committed but its reply was lost, the retry resolves to the same sale instead of charging the customer twice.

    Honest paperwork

    A slip printed offline says the invoice number is pending rather than inventing one. The sequential number is issued by the server the moment the sale lands.

    One platform, different counters

    A hardware shop does not sell like a coffee shop.

    Selling 2.5 metres of chain and selling a cappuccino are different problems. Rather than bend one till to fit every trade, each build starts from how that counter actually works.

    Questions

    The things shops ask first.

    What happens if the internet goes down mid-sale?

    Nothing stops. The catalogue is on the tablet, so the sale completes and the invoice prints. It is stored on the device and synced when the line returns — with a key that prevents the same sale being recorded twice.

    Do we need special hardware?

    No. An Android tablet and a Bluetooth thermal printer. A USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner behaves as a keyboard, so scanning works without any extra integration.

    Is it right for SARS?

    Invoices carry sequential numbers and your VAT registration, and the VAT amount is stored as it was charged, so a reprint years later shows the original figures. If you also trade in Maloti near the border, tell us — that affects how the drawer and cash-up are set up.

    Can we keep our own product codes?

    Yes. Products carry your SKU and, where one exists, a barcode. Import matches on your SKU, so a repeat import updates rather than duplicates.

    Who can see cost prices?

    Only staff you grant it to. Seeing costs is a separate permission from managing products, so a supervisor can fix a price or a barcode without seeing the shop's margins.

    What does it cost?

    It depends on the number of tills and how much setup your price list needs. Get in touch and we will quote against your actual range rather than a generic tier.
